4Write the card

A web page isn't allowed to write to an SD card directly, so this step uses a small free tool on your computer.

  1. Download the release
  2. Install balenaEtcher, open it and choose Flash from file.
  3. Pick the file you downloaded, pick your microSD card, and press Flash. It erases the card.

For macOS and Linux. It only offers removable disks, checks the download and asks before erasing. It needs xz, which macOS gets with brew install xz.

Choose a release first

Then, on the console

  1. Put the card in the first slot and switch the console on. The first boot makes a drive called GAMES, then shows the home screen.
  2. Switch off, put the card back in your computer, and unzip your games into the psp folder on GAMES.

No cookies, no tracking. The release list comes straight from GitHub, which sees your address when your browser asks for it. Games are their authors' work and download from their own pages.
